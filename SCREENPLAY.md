# Glad Purge

FADE IN:

INT. SERVICE TUNNEL - UNDER-GRID SECTOR - NIGHT

A single caged work-light buzzes. Steel floor grating runs with a thin film of water. Three figures lie zip-tied at the ankles and wrists. One is already motionless against the conduit wall, throat open. The other two are still breathing.

The SPEAKER (30s) stands over them. Short blade in his right hand, cracked handheld in his left. His boots are wet. He does not hurry.

SPEAKER
Purge gladly all these enemies of my God.

He glances at the handheld. A feed window is still live.

SPEAKER
Name. Marcus Hale. Chapel map posted zero two fourteen.

The woman nearest him tries to lift her head. Blood strings from her lip to the grating. He does not look down.

SPEAKER
Feed stays live forty seconds. Speak if you still have the next name.

No reply. Only the sump pump kicking on deeper in the tunnel and the steady hum of the work-light. He watches the timer on the handheld reach zero.

SPEAKER
Silence. Tier two logged. Next address queued. Elena Voss. Accelerant confirmed.

He steps across the first body. His boot leaves a partial tread in the cooling blood. He stops over the second figure.

SPEAKER
Feed stays live thirty seconds.

SPEAKER
Twenty.

SPEAKER
Ten.

The second feed drops offline. He cuts the ankle tie with one motion, shifts the limbs so the camera angle would read clean, then moves to the last one.

SPEAKER
Clear. Tier three. Next. David Cho. Feed comment zero two forty-one.

SPEAKER
Twenty seconds.

He waits. The only sound is the pump and the light. When the timer ends he reaches down and kills the final feed himself.

SPEAKER
Silence. Tier three confirmed. Count matches.

He wipes the blade once across the nearest jacket, slides it away, and pockets the handheld. He turns and starts back the way he came. His footsteps fade on the grating.

SPEAKER
Check a tiered impact, deploy a masterpiece for all.

The work-light remains on. The three figures do not move. The pump continues.

FADE OUT.
